## Translation

Worked example (first column): {PT 嚥} is {CM 翁} {CH ⿰口昭} {PT 反}: by the method [they are] combined into the character 嚥. The others follow this; the fanqie method is likewise.

Five further characters follow, each with a Nôm annotation (second column).

## Notes

Transcribed by Claude as in [E07](E07.md); notation as in the [evidence README](README.md).

### Findings

- The combined character at the top of the first column is, according to the proposer, the unmarked Cán of ONG on the left and the Chi MARKED-5 of CHONG on the right, read yên (嚥). It has no tone mark. The marked Cán of ONG in the formula carries a tone mark ([E34](E34.md)); the Chi MARKED-5 of CHONG in the formula carries a semicircle at the lower left. A Cán on the left and a Chi of another base on the right: the phản thiết arrangement ([E06](E06.md)).
- The formula has the same form as the worked example in [E07](E07.md), with the closing formula 他倣此切法亦然.

### Open

- Placement: the passage has no heading and does not fit the Hợp Thư heading, because it shows a Cán on the left and a Chi on the right. The pages are not in the wrong order. The last column of p. 6 ends at about 40 per cent of the page height, which marks the end of the section before ([E07](E07.md)); a double page may be missing (proposer's observation). Whether the passage belongs to the section 反切諧音法 ([E05](E05.md)) or to another unit is not established.
- Annotations that are not certain: ⿰口昭 (first column), ⿰口淵 and 卬 (second column, first and fourth).
- Composition of the five characters in the second column (the proposer's hint: the Chi of CHONG run through the positions, next to the unmarked Cán of ONG); not transcribed in detail.
