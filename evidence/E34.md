## Notes

Stated by the proposer from the whole text; the listing of the Cán (E18) and the tone examples (E15) show both forms standalone.

### Findings

- Outside the listing of the 22 Cán (p. 2, E18) the marked Cán occurs only twice, both times in the chapter on the Tham Thư. There it is used as a separate character with a tone and a reading, not as an abstract entry of a list (stated by the proposer; pages and columns to be added). It does not combine there.

### Open

- Pages and columns of the two occurrences.
- Proposer's hypothesis, not yet researched: the marking could identify the head word, which would explain why the listing of the 22 Cán shows the marked form. Not used in the proposal.
