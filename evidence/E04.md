## Notes

Notation: {CL 翁} = standalone Cán Lược annotated 翁, {CT …} = standalone Chi Tự, {PT …} = phản thiết pair (Cán Lược + Chi Tự in one character); {PT 反} = the syllable 反 written in QATT as a phản thiết pair, followed by the same syllable in Han. Shows a standalone Cán Lược before a Chi Tự (翁, 宜) and two adjacent Cán Lược (翁, 翁), none combined, next to combined phản thiết pairs. Translation to be added. Tone marks: every QATT character and component in the passage carries the mark of the neutral tone (ngang) except the last two, {PT 衣}{PT 字}, which carry none.
