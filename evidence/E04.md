## Notes

Notation as in the [evidence README](README.md): `{CL …}` standalone Cán Lược, `{CT …}` standalone Chi Tự, `{PT …}` phản thiết pair; the character after the type is the annotation. Positions are counted from the top of the column.

### Findings

- **Positions 7–8:** a standalone Cán Lược (翁) before a standalone Chi Tự (宜), not combined.
- **Positions 9–10:** the syllable 反 written first in QATT as a phản thiết pair, then as a Han character.
- **Positions 12–14:** two standalone Cán Lược (翁, 翁) before a phản thiết pair (鳥). As a bare component sequence this is CL CL CL CT; only the joiner shows which Cán Lược combines.
- **Tone marks:** every QATT character and component carries the mark of the neutral tone (ngang), except the last two, {PT 衣}{PT 字}, which carry none (E24–E26).
