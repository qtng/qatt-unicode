## Notes

The section on the Hợp Thư follows directly on the section 反切諧音法 (E05) and runs from p. 6, sixth column, to p. 7, second column. Reading of the heading confirmed by the proposer; transcription and translation still to be added.

### Findings

- The count section on p. 8 (E36) counts 880 characters with the Chi on the left and the Cán on the right, that is 110 pairs × 8 tones. The 110 pairs are exactly the same-base pairs (22 × 5). This confirms the narrow reading (proposer's decision); the broad reading is refuted.
- The same section also applies the word 合書 to the arrangement with the Cán on the left (18,480 = 2,310 × 8), which covers only pairs of different bases.
