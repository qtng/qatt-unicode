## Notes

### Terminology

- The source gives the form without the marking stroke no name and no separate description. The term Cán Lược does not occur in it (checked by the proposer throughout both manuscripts). The proposal calls the form the abbreviated Cán, an English description, and adds ABBREVIATED to the name of the Cán Tự in character names.
- The source calls both the Cán Tự and the abbreviated Cán 幹 (E36).
- Two section headings end in 畧: 合書左從枝体右從幹体畧法 (p. 6, E07) and 參書下從合体上從枝体畧 (p. 7, E10). In the second, the character before 体 is 枝, not 幹, in both sources. Whether 畧 describes a component or closes the heading ("brief method") is not established, so the headings are not used for terminology.
