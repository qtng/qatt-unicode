## Notes

### Terminology

- The source does not name the form without the marking stroke. The proposer found the description 幹体畧 (cán thể lược), "abbreviated trunk body"; passage to be cited. The proposal calls the form the abbreviated Cán and, in character names, adds ABBREVIATED to the name of the Cán Tự.
- The term Cán Lược was checked by the proposer throughout both manuscripts and does not occur as a term. The source calls both the Cán Tự and the abbreviated Cán 幹 (E36). The heading of the section on the Tham Thư contains the similar expression 枝体畧 (p. 7, E10).
