## Notes

### Terminology

- The source gives the form without the marking stroke no name and no separate description. The term Cán Lược does not occur in it (checked by the proposer throughout both manuscripts). The proposal calls the form the unmarked Cán, a description of the form; in character names it is UNMARKED, and the marked forms are MARKED-1 to MARKED-6 by the position of the marking stroke.
- The source calls both the marked Cán and the unmarked Cán 幹 (E36).
- Two section headings end in 畧: 合書左從枝体右從幹体畧法 (p. 6, E07) and 參書下從合体上從枝体畧 (p. 7, E10). In the second, the character before 体 is 枝, not 幹, in both sources. Whether 畧 describes a component or closes the heading ("brief method") is not established, so the headings are not used for terminology.
