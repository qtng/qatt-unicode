## Notes

### Terminology

- The term Cán Lược does not occur in the source; checked by the proposer throughout both manuscripts. The source calls both the Cán Tự and the Cán Lược 幹 (E36). The only similar expression is in the heading of the section on the Tham Thư, 參書下從合体上從枝体畧 (p. 7, E10). Origin of the term: open.
