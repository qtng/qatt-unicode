## Transcription

本字八音識別法。於本字上依陰陽四象識別，每一字各成八字。如同東合一字，惟同音其聲在上為陽，東音其聲在下為陰。以陰陽分配，則東董凍篤四音是陰之平上去入，同洞動獨四音是陽之平上去入。以四象識別，則陽用全圈，陰用半圈。

## Translation

Method of distinguishing the eight tones of a character: on the character, distinguish by yin and yang and the four images (四象), so that each character forms eight characters. For example, 同 and 東 are combined in one character; but the sound of 同 is above and is yang, the sound of 東 is below and is yin. Divided by yin and yang, the four sounds 東 董 凍 篤 are the level, rising, departing and entering tones of yin, and the four sounds 同 洞 動 獨 are the level, rising, departing and entering tones of yang. Distinguished by the four images, yang uses a full circle and yin a half circle. [Draft translation, to be checked]

## Notes

Eight-tone yin-yang system confirmed by the passage: full circle for dương, half circle for âm. The corners are not named in the passage (四象, 'four images'). In Source 2 the scribe first wrote 平去上入 for yin and marked 去上 for transposition (C02).
