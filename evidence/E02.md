## Transcription

枝字一百一十字 (see [E29](E29.md))

## Notes

The evidence is the table of the Chi ([E29](E29.md)). It lists the forms in blocks of five per Cán, 22 blocks of five, 109 forms shown. Within a block the forms differ by the position of the marking stroke; the marking stroke sits only at stroke ends, which gives six positions, one of them the position of the marked Cán ([E01](E01.md)), so five are left for the Chi.

The table shows 109 forms, but its heading names 110 (枝字一百一十字). The missing form is an error of the author, not a feature of the system: 22 × 5 = 110, and the blocks of five follow the same pattern throughout ([E29](E29.md)).

The rule in the claim can be read directly on the images: the marking stroke runs counter-clockwise from one form to the next within a block (stated by the proposer). A recorded check of all 22 blocks against the rule is not part of this entry.

The images show p. 3 and p. 4 in full (Source 1). On p. 4 the columns that do not belong to the table, the section on the tones ([E14](E14.md)), are framed and crossed out.
